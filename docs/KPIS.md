# Which numbers to watch, and why

Every number below is on the dashboard, counted by code from your platform. Ordered by how often you should look.

## Every few minutes (the Live floor)

| Number | What it means | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **On a call / waiting / blocked** | Who is live right now | Calls that find nobody online go to someone else. That money is lost. |
| **Script bar** (Greet → Right call → Not free → Age → Bank) | How far each live call got in the qualifying script | A caller who passes all 5 is a real buyer. Green = passed, red = failed. |
| **Seconds to billable** | Countdown to the billing mark (90 s by default) | Calls ended right before it cost your agents and burn your publishers. |
| **Flags** | Agent asked for the caller's number, caller outside 50 to 85 | The two things that start disputes and lose publishers. |
| **Wallet / calls left** | Each agent's money ÷ their price per call | At 0 calls left, they stop getting calls even when they're online. |
| **Platform balance** | Money on your call platform for talk time | At $0, **every** call is refused. |

## Every day (Overview)

| Number | What it means | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Margin** | What agents paid − what you owe publishers | The money the calls made you. |
| **Bottom line (net)** | Margin − talk time − platform fee − ads | What you actually kept. |
| **Billable rate** | Billed calls ÷ calls in | Low = bad traffic, the wrong agents, or agents cutting calls. |
| **Offers taken** | Offers accepted ÷ offers sent to you | Refused offers are calls you could have sold. The reasons tell you why (nobody online, state not covered). |
| **Needs you now** | The short list of things to act on | Built from the numbers, never written by hand. |

## Every week (Make more money)

| Number | What it means | Do this |
|---|---|---|
| **Money missed** | Calls with no agent online × your usual bill rate | Get one more agent live in your busiest hours. |
| **Agents about to run dry** | Can't afford the next call, or have one left | Text them to top up today. |
| **Money loaded, never used** | Paid agents who never took a call | One phone call to get them logged in. |
| **Best hour** | The hour with the highest billable rate | Ask your best agents to be online then. |
| **Bill rate by publisher** | Which publisher's calls bill most | Send more volume where it bills. |
| **Cost per booked call / per paying agent** | Ad spend ÷ bookings, ÷ new paying agents | Which ads actually make money. |
